SECTION 1: FEATURES
================================================================================

- Common-mode range (VCM): -0.2 V to +26 V
- High bandwidth: 350 kHz (A1 devices)
- Offset voltage:
  - +/-150 uV (maximum) at VCM = 0 V
  - +/-500 uV (maximum) at VCM = 12 V
- Output slew rate: 2 V/us
- Accuracy:
  - +/-1% gain error (maximum)
  - 1-uV/C offset drift (maximum)
- Gain options:
  - 20 V/V (A1 devices)
  - 50 V/V (A2 devices)
  - 100 V/V (A3 devices)
  - 200 V/V (A4 devices)
- Quiescent current: 260 uA maximum (INA180)

================================================================================
SECTION 3: DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

The INA180, INA2180, and INA4180 (INAx180) current sense amplifiers are designed
for cost-optimized applications. These devices are part of a family of current-sense
amplifiers (also called current-shunt monitors) that sense voltage drops across
current-sense resistors at common-mode voltages from -0.2 V to +26 V, independent
of the supply voltage. The INAx180 integrate a matched resistor gain network in
four, fixed-gain device options: 20 V/V, 50 V/V, 100 V/V, or 200 V/V. This matched
gain resistor network minimizes gain error and reduces the temperature drift.

All these devices operate from a single 2.7-V to 5.5-V power supply. The single-
channel INA180 draws a maximum supply current of 260 uA; whereas, the dual-channel
INA2180 draws a maximum supply current of 500 uA, and the quad channel draws a
maximum supply current of 900 uA.

The INA180 is available in a 5-pin, SOT-23 package with two different pin
configurations. The INA2180 is available in 8-pin, VSSOP and WSON packages. The
INA4180 is available in a 14-pin, TSSOP package. All device options are specified
over the extended operating temperature range of -40C to +125C.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: DETAILED DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

8.1 Overview:
The INA180, INA2180, and INA4180 (INAx180) are 26-V, common-mode, current-sensing
amplifiers used in both low-side and high-side configurations.

8.3.1 High Bandwidth and Slew Rate:
Small-signal bandwidths as high as 350 kHz, large-signal slew rates of 2 V/us.

8.3.2 Wide Input Common-Mode Voltage Range:
-0.2 V to +26 V. Common-mode range is not restricted by the power-supply voltage (VS)
as long as VS stays within 2.7 V to 5.5 V.

8.3.3 Precise Low-Side Current Sensing:
Offset voltage within +/-150 uV. Gain error within 1%.

8.3.4 Rail-to-Rail Output Swing:
Max swing to positive rail: 30 mV. Max swing to GND: 5 mV.

8.4 Device Functional Modes:
- Normal mode: VS = 2.7-5.5 V, VCM = -0.2 to +26 V
- Input differential overload: output driven to rail, recovers ~20 us after fault
- Shutdown mode: no dedicated shutdown pin, use external switch on VS

================================================================================
SECTION 9: APPLICATION AND IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

9.1.1 Basic Connections:
- Connect IN+ and IN- as close as possible to shunt resistor (Kelvin connection)
- Bypass capacitor: 0.1 uF minimum on VS pin

9.1.2 RSENSE and Device Gain Selection:
- Input resistance (RINT) by gain version:
    A1 (20 V/V):  25 kohm
    A2 (50 V/V):  10 kohm
    A3 (100 V/V):  5 kohm
    A4 (200 V/V): 2.5 kohm

9.3 Power Supply Recommendations:
- VS can be 5 V while bus voltage at IN+/IN- can be up to 26 V
- Output voltage range limited by VS
- Device withstands full 26 V differential at inputs even without power

9.4 Layout Guidelines:
- Use Kelvin (4-wire) connection to sense resistor
- Place bypass cap as close as possible to VS and GND pins
- Keep input trace lengths matched
